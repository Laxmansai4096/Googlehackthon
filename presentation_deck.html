03 / 10

Why Traditional Supply Chains Fail in Rural Clinics

Three structural systemic bottlenecks prevent timely medicine delivery.

📝 1. The Data Entry Trap
Rural pharmacists in rural dispensaries manage up to 200 OPD patients daily. They lack the time to type 20 fields (batch, expiry, manufacturer) into cumbersome central web portals.

Result: Stock registers remain paper-bound, creating complete digital blindness at the district level.
🏛️ 2. Bureaucratic Restock Inertia
When a clinic exhausts an essential drug, restocking currently requires manual paper requisition, physical signatures, and warehouse dispatch.

Result: Rebalancing takes 7 to 14 days, far too slow for an acute venom or postpartum hemorrhage emergency.
🔒 3. State Data Sovereignty Walls
Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, state health missions cannot legally centralize raw citizen electronic health records into a single monolithic server.

Result: Predictive disease outbreak models cannot cross state borders without federated privacy.
